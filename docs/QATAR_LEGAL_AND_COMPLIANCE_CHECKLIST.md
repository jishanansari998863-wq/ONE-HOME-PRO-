# Qatar Business, Tax & Data Privacy Compliance Checklist (ONE HOME PRO)

> **Important Legal Notice**: Software readiness does NOT replace legal counsel or statutory registration. Items marked **Pending External Verification** require verification by your licensed Qatar legal/tax advisor and company management prior to commercial launch.

| Jurisdiction / Body | Requirement | App Support Implemented | External / Governance Action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qatar MOCI** (Ministry of Commerce & Industry) | Valid Commercial Registration (CR) & Municipality Trade License on company profile & receipts | Company CR field stored on `BusinessEntity` and rendered on Official Receipts & Owner Control Center | Enter real CR number & upload CR copy into Document Vault | Pending External Verification |
| **Qatar Municipality** (Baladiya / Lease Registration) | Property lease registration compliance for residential/commercial tenancies | Structured Agreements, Room numbers, Property details, Notice period & Tenant QID/Passport storage | Register eligible lease contracts via official Municipality portal | Pending External Verification |
| **Qatar Law No. 13 of 2016** (Personal Data Privacy Protection Law) | Lawful purpose, minimal data collection, access audit logging, erasure/closure workflow | RBAC least-privilege isolation, private app vault for QID/Passport with SHA-256 + `document_access_logs`, Customer Account Closure Request workflow | Publish company Arabic/English Privacy Policy & designate DPO | Implemented Technically; Governance Pending |
| **QCB / Banking Controls** | Accurate cheque & payment tracking (no uncleared PDC counted as bank balance) | Cheques tracked via `1020 Cheques Receivable`; Bank (`1010`) increases only upon explicit `CLEARED` transition | Reconcile physical bank statements monthly via `Bank Reconciliation` screen | Implemented |
| **Qatar Tax / GTA & E-Invoicing** | Sequential numbered receipts, audit trail, non-erasable reversed journals | Sequential `RCP-*`, `PAY-*`, `JE-*` numbering; reversals create contra-entries rather than deleting posted vouchers | Confirm applicable tax / audit retention schedule (typically 10 years) with Qatar CPA | Implemented Technically; External CPA Review Pending |
| **Google Play Developer Policies** | Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`) / SAF instead of broad `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`; short non-promotional app name; no DCL; in-app account closure flow | Zero storage permissions in `AndroidManifest.xml`; `PickVisualMedia` & `OpenDocument` used; closure request screen included | Complete Play Console Data Safety form, Privacy Policy URL, and Google Play review | Technically Compliant; Store Review Pending |
